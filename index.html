by Joe Samad
An insightful read into the relationship between Sabah and Sarawak, and their partners.

One of the books that reignited my passion for reading into my adulthood. The author describes history in a way that makes you feel like you are part of it, too.

A romcom that follows Maddy, who has an autoimmune disease, as she falls in love with her neighbour, Olly.
